# General

## What is GoodHive?
GoodHive is a Web3-native talent marketplace that connects businesses with skilled professionals in the decentralized economy. We use blockchain technology to create transparent, secure, and efficient hiring processes.

## How does GoodHive work?
Companies can post jobs and browse talent profiles, while professionals can showcase their skills and apply for opportunities. All interactions are secured by smart contracts, ensuring transparent and trustless transactions.

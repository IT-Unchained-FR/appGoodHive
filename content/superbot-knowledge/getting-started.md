# Getting Started

## Do I need cryptocurrency to use GoodHive?
While we operate on blockchain technology, you don't need extensive crypto knowledge to get started. We provide user-friendly interfaces and support to help you navigate the Web3 ecosystem.

## How do I get started as a talent?
Simply create your profile, showcase your skills and experience, upload your resume, and start browsing opportunities. Our AI-powered matching system will help connect you with relevant positions.

## How do I post a job as a company?
Register your company profile, describe your job requirements, set your budget, and publish your posting. You'll start receiving applications from qualified candidates right away.
